## Systematic Error Analysis

### Error #1: Shutout Bias (SEVERE)
**Pattern:** Predicted 5 shutouts (Norway vs Iraq, Argentina vs Algeria, Austria vs Jordan, Portugal vs Congo, Colombia vs Uzbekistan). Only 1 materialized (Argentina vs Algeria).

- Shutout prediction accuracy: **1/5 = 20%**
- In all 4 failed shutout predictions, the "weaker" team scored exactly 1 goal
- This directly caused BTTS accuracy to collapse to 37.5%

**Root cause:** Overweighting pre-tournament defensive form for favorites when facing World Cup debutants/underdogs with high motivation. World Cup openers almost always feature an underdog goal from pride/momentum/set pieces.

### Error #2: Goal Tally Underestimation (MODERATE)
**Pattern:** In matches where the favorite won big, actual goal totals exceeded predictions:
- Norway: predicted 3, scored 4
- Argentina: predicted 2, scored 3
- England: predicted 3 total, actual was 6
- Colombia: predicted 2, scored 3

**Root cause:** Conservative bias in predicting dominant team's ceiling. Strong favorites in group stage openers often score more than "reasonable" predictions suggest.

### Error #3: O/U Directional Errors on "Controlled" Matches
**Pattern:** Predicted Under 2.5 for Argentina, England, and Colombia — all wrong. All three games went Over.

**Root cause:** Conflating "controlled/professional win" with "low-scoring." A dominant team winning 3-0, 4-2, 3-1 is still "controlled" but scores high.

### Error #4: Portugal Overconfidence (SEVERE, isolated)
**Pattern:** The largest single miss. 3-0 prediction when actual was 1-1. DR Congo's known defensive solidity (0.56 goals/game) was flagged but not weighted correctly in the final call.

**Root cause:** The -2.5 handicap bet was selected despite noting the "顽强抵抗" (tenacious resistance) risk. The evidence was in the analysis but the conclusion ignored it. High favorite probability (~77%) should not translate to -2.5 handicap confidence.
